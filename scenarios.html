Proactive Cash Flow & Liquidity Rebalancing

Intelligent Balance Forecasting & Overdraft (LIS) Preemption

CASH_FLOW_FORECAST_AGENT

Customer Context & Trigger

The client schedules or pays a large condominium bill via Pix (R$ 3,850.00). The agent's time-series cash flow forecasting model projects that scheduled automatic debits over the next 4 days (credit card bill and property tax) will cause the checking balance to dip to -R$ 1,420.00, triggering high-interest overdraft fees (LIS).

Autonomous Agentic Execution

  • The Cash Flow Forecasting Agent calculates the exact D+4 projected shortfall.
  • The Treasury & Yield Optimizer detects that the customer holds R$ 85,000.00 in Daily Liquidity CDB DI earning 100% CDI.
  • Calculates the precise optimal redemption schedule so funds remain invested and earning interest until the exact hour of settlement.
  • Sends a proactive smartphone push notification: "We forecast scheduled debits on Aug 25 will require R$ 1,500.00. Would you like us to automatically rebalance from your CDB to avoid overdraft fees?"
Regulatory & Compliance Mandates:

Complies with Central Bank Resolution CMN 4.765 (overdraft interest transparency) with explicit, verifiable customer opt-in prior to asset movement.

Real-Time Pix Anomaly & Emergency MED Protocol

Precautionary Pix Interception & BACEN MED Protocol in <200ms

BACEN_MED_DISPUTE_AGENT

Customer Context & Trigger

A high-value Pix transfer of R$ 4,200.00 is initiated late at night via an authenticated mobile device accessing from a foreign VPN proxy, featuring an anomalous typing cadence, sent to a newly registered recipient key with zero prior transaction history.

Autonomous Agentic Execution

  • The Fraud Monitor Agent places an instant precautionary hold on outgoing funds across SPI rails in <180ms.
  • The Device & Geo Validator triggers voice biometric verification with the Gemini Live assistant.
  • If unverified by the client, it automatically triggers the BACEN MED Desk, filing an official claim under Resolution 147 and reversing the R$ 4,200.00 back to checking within 10 seconds.
  • Temporarily suspends active Apple Pay / Google Pay tokens as a preventive safeguard.
Regulatory & Compliance Mandates:

Central Bank Resolution BCB No. 147/2021 (Special Return Mechanism - MED) and Circular BCB 3.978 (Anti-Money Laundering & Digital Fraud Prevention).

Open Finance Portability & Rate Optimization

Automated Debt Portability & Rate Optimization via Open Finance

OPEN_FINANCE_OPTIMIZER

Customer Context & Trigger

Via Open Finance Brasil telemetry consent, the agent identifies that the customer carries an outstanding revolving credit card balance of R$ 18,000.00 at a competing institution with an annual APR of 240.5% (11.2% per month).

Autonomous Agentic Execution

  • The Open Finance Intelligence Agent pulls external loan registry data from BACEN SCR Registrato.
  • The Credit Underwriting Agent constructs an Itaú Sob Medida consolidation offer at 1.69% per month, reducing the customer's monthly payment by R$ 680.40.
  • Generates an electronic CCB (Cédula de Crédito Bancário) and interbank settlement request via CIP/STR rails.
  • Presents an interactive comparison card in the app with direct one-tap biometric signing via Gov.br / Face ID.
Regulatory & Compliance Mandates:

Joint Resolution No. 1/2020 (Open Finance Brasil FAPI), Law 10.931 (Electronic CCB Issuance & Registration), and LGPD Art. 20 (Algorithmic Decision Explainability).

Corporate Cash Flow & Receivables Discounting (PJ)

Autonomous Receivables Advance & Working Capital Liquidity (PJ)

PJ_WORKING_CAPITAL_AGENT

Business Context & Trigger

A commercial banking corporate client (Itaú Empresas) has a payroll settlement of R$ 65,000.00 due tomorrow, with only R$ 22,000.00 available in checking, but holds R$ 58,400.00 in credit card receivables registered in CERC/B3 due in 15 to 30 days.

Autonomous Agentic Execution

  • The Working Capital Agent inspects CERC / TAG registrars to identify unencumbered Receivable Units (URs).
  • Applies an institutional preferential discounting rate (1.15% per month) with automatic collateral lock, calculating the exact net amount needed to cover payroll without unnecessary debt.
  • Sends a one-tap notification to the CFO on the Itaú Empresas app: "We detected an upcoming R$ 43,000 payroll shortfall. Advance your card sales at 1.15%/mo with instant Pix disbursement in 1 minute?"
  • Executes the fiduciary assignment on the central registry and disburses funds instantly via Pix SPI.
Regulatory & Compliance Mandates:

Circular BCB No. 3.952 (Payment Scheme Receivables Registration) and Resolution BCB No. 105 (Collateral Pledges over Receivable Units).
