Official Design Standards • Itaú Personnalité & Itaú Empresas

The Swiss Banking Design System

A comprehensive design system and visual token kit engineered for executive authority, cryptographic clarity, and zero-fatigue high-density financial data. Built on high contrast, strict tabular alignment, mathematical centering, and restrained semantic color.

Principle 01

Swiss Tabular Precision

Rigid two-column alignment, zero text wrapping, and mathematical centering. Financial figures use dedicated tabular monospace fonts.

Principle 02

Semantic Color Exclusivity

Green is strictly semantic for savings and verified status. Core orange represents Itaú brand identity. Extraneous blue and purple are eliminated.

Principle 03

Turn-One Directness

Executive banking poise requires instant clarity. No questionnaire stalling, no banner toast popups, and no ambiguous multi-step menus.

Executive Voice Profile

Voice & Spoken Persona Directives

Honorific & Respect

ALWAYS address the customer as "Mr. Silva" in English and "Sr. Silva" in Portuguese. Never use casual first names like "Robert".

Currency Pronunciation

NEVER say "dollars" or convert figures. Say "Reais" (ray-ICE) or "Real" (ray-AL). E.g. "48.950 reais".

Brand Pronunciation

Pronounce "Itaú" as ee-tah-OO and "Personnalité" as pehr-soh-nah-lee-TAY.

Executive Brevity & Directness

Directly answer questions on turn 1. Maximum 2–3 sentences per turn. Never stall with multiple-choice questionnaires when asked for balance.

Brand Guardrails

System Do's & Don'ts

✓ Strict Brand Do's
  • Enforce Swiss Tabular Alignment: Always align competitor data in clean side-by-side columns.
  • Use Monospace for Numerics: Currency, percentages, and timestamps must use monospace fonts.
  • Reserve Green for Semantics: Use green exclusively for net savings, positive spreads, and verified security.
  • Direct First-Turn Responses: State checking balance (R$ 48.950,20) immediately when queried.
✗ Strict Brand Don'ts
  • NO Blue: Do not introduce blue (#003399, #3B82F6) in gradients, buttons, or graphs.
  • NO Purple: Do not use purple or violet in scenarios or telemetry badges.
  • NO Popups / Toasts: Do not display sliding push banners over the smartphone interface.
  • NO Questionnaire Menus: Never present a 1/2/3 multiple-choice clarification card for balance queries.
