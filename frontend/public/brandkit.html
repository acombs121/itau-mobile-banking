Official Design Standards • Itaú Personnalité & Itaú Empresas

The Swiss Banking Design System

An exhaustive design token and component specification engineered for executive authority, zero-fatigue high-density financial data, and mathematical alignment. Built upon cryptographic clarity, rigorous tabular monospace numerics, and restrained semantic color.

Principle 01

Swiss Tabular Precision

Rigid two-column alignment, zero text wrapping, and mathematical centering. All financial numbers enforce tabular proportional figures (tnum).

Principle 02

Semantic Color Exclusivity

Green is strictly semantic for savings, yields, and verified states. Core orange is the sole brand signature. Extraneous decorative blues and purples are strictly prohibited.

Principle 03

Turn-One Directness

Executive banking poise requires immediate clarity. No questionnaire stalling, no banner toast popups, and no multi-step menus when an account balance is queried.

Executive Voice Profile

Voice & Spoken Persona Directives

Honorific & Respect

ALWAYS address the customer as "Mr. Silva" in English and "Sr. Silva" in Portuguese. Never use casual first names like "Robert".

Currency Pronunciation

NEVER say "dollars" or convert figures. Say "Reais" (Ray-Ice) or "Real" (Ray-AHL). Any plural Real figure MUST be pronounced "Ray-Ice", not "Ray-AHL". E.g. "48.950 reais" pronounced with "Ray-Ice".

Brand Pronunciation

Pronounce "Itaú" as ee-tah-OO and "Personnalité" as pehr-soh-nah-lee-TAY.

Executive Brevity & Directness

Directly answer balance inquiries on turn 1 (stating the R$ 48.950,20 checking balance). Maximum 2–3 sentences per turn. Never stall with questionnaires.

Brand Guardrails

System Do's & Don'ts

✓ Strict Brand Do's
  • Enforce Swiss Tabular Alignment: Always align competitor data in clean side-by-side columns with zero wrapping.
  • Use Monospace for Numerics: Currency, percentages, and timestamps must use JetBrains Mono / SF Mono.
  • Reserve Green for Semantics: Use green exclusively for net savings, positive spreads, and verified security.
  • Direct First-Turn Responses: State checking balance (R$ 48.950,20) immediately when queried.
✗ Strict Brand Don'ts
  • NO Blue: Do not introduce blue (#003399, #3B82F6) in gradients, buttons, or graphs.
  • NO Purple: Do not use purple or violet in scenarios or telemetry badges.
  • NO Popups / Toasts: Do not display sliding push banners over the smartphone interface.
  • NO Questionnaire Menus: Never present a 1/2/3 multiple-choice clarification card for balance queries.
