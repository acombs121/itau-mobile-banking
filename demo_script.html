1. Proactive Value vs Reactive Chat

The AI doesn't wait to be asked. It calculates future cash shortfalls and monitors external Open Finance telemetry to protect the customer before fees or friction happen.

LLMs generate the natural conversational voice, but all financial actions (CDB sweeps, travel limit elevations, CCB loan contracts) are executed deterministically through governed sub-agents.

By capturing high-interest external loans and consolidating them into Itaú Personnalité, the bank gains loan book volume while delivering substantial verified savings to the client.
